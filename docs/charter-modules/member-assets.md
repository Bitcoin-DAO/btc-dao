## [n] · Member assets

For assets a member buys through [DAO name] and owns themselves, such as a solar panel at a shared site ("[asset name]", for example a sunblock).

1. **Not a gift.** Unlike a donation (Article 2), a payment towards a member asset buys something the member will own. It gives no rights over the rest of the treasury and no extra votes.
2. **Pledge.** A member asks for a [asset name]. The app reserves a treasury receive address for the pledge, and the member pays the price into it in sats, at once or in instalments. Payments to that address count only towards that pledge.
3. **Price.** The price is the cost of the asset and its share of installation, in sats, fixed when the purchase proposal passes. [Choose one: if the price rises before then, the member tops up / the treasury covers the difference.] Any surplus goes back to the member.
4. **Purchase.** When a pledge is covered, any member proposes the purchase: a payout to the supplier. It is voted on like any other payment.
5. **Ownership.** After installation, a statement proposal records the asset, its owner and the pledge that funded it. Once passed, that record is the proof of ownership. The member owns the asset outright: [DAO name] looks after it at the site and charges no fees.
6. **Return.** Each [asset name] adds to its owner's share of the DAO's income, as set in the distribution rule [in the settings or a further module].
7. **Leaving.** A member who leaves may take their asset, sell it to another member, or sell it back to [DAO name] at a price the members approve. Until one of these happens, it keeps earning for its owner.
8. **Capacity and refunds.** [DAO name] accepts pledges only while the site has room for another [asset name]; others wait in order. If an asset cannot be bought within [period], the pledge is refunded in full by a payout proposal.
9. **Who may pledge.** [Members only / anyone, who becomes a member on purchase.]
