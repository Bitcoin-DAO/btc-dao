# How members get sats

For members who want to pay dues, lend to the DAO or donate, and have only euros (or pounds) today.

**The DAO does not sell bitcoin.** It never takes fiat, never changes money, and no member does it on the DAO's behalf. This is the same rule as on the way out: the DAO pays only in bitcoin, and an Agent handles the fiat side of a purchase as their own business ([action gateway](drafts/action-gateway.md), section 3.4). Each member gets their own sats, in their own name, by a route they choose.

## 1. First, a wallet of your own

Before buying anything, set up a wallet you control: [Your first bitcoin wallet in 5 minutes](wallet-guide.en.md) ([Italiano](wallet-guide.it.md)). Buy into **your** wallet, then send from it to the DAO. Never buy straight to the treasury address: if anything goes wrong, you want the coins to be yours to fix.

## 2. Routes

| Route | How it works | Good for | Watch out for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Exchange or broker app** | Pay by bank transfer or card in an app, then withdraw to your own wallet | Most people; regular dues | Choose one registered to operate in your country. It will ask for ID. Withdraw to your wallet: do not leave coins on the app |
| **Bitcoin ATM** | Cash in, sats to a wallet QR | Small, occasional amounts | High fees; check the machine's rate before paying |
| **Peer to peer** | Buy from a person, face to face or through a P2P group or app | People who prefer not to use an exchange | Meet in a public place or use a platform with escrow; never send fiat first to a stranger |
| **Earn it** | Be paid by the DAO for work: a payout, a bounty, an Agent task | Members who contribute work | Payouts need an address from your own wallet (a fresh one each time) |

**On a testnet DAO** (such as ALPHA DAO), members rehearse the peer-to-peer route with tBTC: one person sells, no money changes hands, the tBTC goes to the buyer's standing address, and the buyer pays their dues from that coin. It earns the buyer the Paid-up badge. It is practice for the route, not a DAO service.

**From another member?** A one-off private sale between two people is their own business, outside the DAO. A member who sells sats to other members as a regular service starts to look like a crypto exchange business, which is regulated in the EU and the UK. The DAO does not set up such a role, and its record does not list such sales.

## 3. Paying the DAO

1. Open the DAO's site and go to the treasury page. The treasury address is shown there, with its QR code.
2. Check the address on the site matches the one read out or shown at the meeting, at least the first time.
3. Send from your own wallet. **Dues:** pay the dues amount from the coin at your standing address (the address on your profile); the app then sees it was you, with nothing else to do. **A loan or a pledge:** the payment is recorded against the proposal it belongs to (post the txid where the app asks for it).

**Nobody from the DAO will ever ask you to pay anywhere else:** not by email, not in the chat, not on a call. The only address is the one in the app. If someone asks, tell the council.

## 4. Small print

- **Fees.** Every route takes a cut, and sending on chain has a network fee. For small dues, paying a few months at once costs less.
- **Price.** Dues are set in sats, so the euro cost changes with the price. That is the point of a bitcoin DAO, but members should know it before they agree a dues amount.
- **Tax.** Buying, holding and spending bitcoin is taxed differently from country to country. The DAO does not advise on it; ask someone qualified where you live.
