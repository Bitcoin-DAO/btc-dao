# DAO onboarding kit

November 2026

Everything a group needs to understand, found and run a Bitcoin DAO, in the order people need it. Each stage lists who it is for and which documents to read. Most of the kit already exists; the gaps are listed at the end.

The kit will become the "public beta: help and onboarding" docs site, in English and Italian.

## 1. Curious: what is a DAO?

**For:** anyone hearing about it for the first time.

- [What is a DAO? One-page handout](handout.md) ([Italiano](handout.it.md)): for events and first contact. A summary of the page below; when that page changes, check the handout.
- [What is a Bitcoin DAO?](what-is-a-bitcoin-dao.md) ([Italiano](what-is-a-bitcoin-dao.it.md)): what a DAO is, how it works, what it can be used for, how it compares with a co-op (including running costs), and what it is not.

## 2. Deciding: should our group start one?

**For:** the people who would found it.

- [Funding models](funding-models.md): where the money will come from once the first donations are spent. Read and discuss before drafting the mainnet charter.
- [Charter template](charter-template.en.md): the short core charter every DAO starts from.
- [Charter modules](charter-modules/README.md): optional articles (council handbook, council eligibility, use of funds, conduct, language, personal data, member assets, trial phase).

## 3. Preparing: getting ready to found

**For:** the organiser and the council.

- [Starting a new DAO: checklist](new-dao.md): everything to prepare, in order, from the first decisions to the days after founding.
- [The complete guide](guide.md): roles, the three stages (practice, testnet, mainnet), council ceremonies, the founding meeting, everyday running, changes and closing.
- [BTC DAO Testnet Treasury](testnet-treasury.md): where the test coins for the practice and testnet stages come from.

## 4. Founding day

**For:** the council and the founding members.

- [Council key workshop](workshop/README.md): how the council makes its keys together, before the founding meeting.
- [Founding day run sheet](workshop/run-sheet.md): one page for the room: roles, timings and the three checks.
- Workshop cards: printed with [`make_cards.py`](workshop/make_cards.py) ([sample](workshop/sample-cards.pdf)).

## 5. Holding keys and receiving payments

**For:** council members, and anyone receiving a payout.

- [SeedSigner formats](seedsigner.md): what the app reads from and shows to a SeedSigner.
- [Your first bitcoin wallet in 5 minutes](wallet-guide.en.md) ([Italiano](wallet-guide.it.md)): for members who need an address to receive a payment.
- [How members get sats](getting-sats.md): for members who want to pay dues, lend or donate and have only fiat today. The DAO does not sell bitcoin; this lists the routes members use themselves.

## 6. Growing

**For:** members and the council, once the DAO is running.

- [Tiers, stages and badges](proposals/badges.md) (proposal): the badges members and DAOs earn, and the competence path from a first council to supporting child DAOs.
- Network funding (design stage): the [GRANTS DAO and repayable grants](drafts/grants-dao.md), and [member loans to the DAO](drafts/member-loans.md).

## Gaps

- **Step-by-step council and member onboarding,** with screenshots. Waits until after the ALPHA DAO workshop and any changes it brings, so the screenshots match the app (`NEXT-RELEASE.md`, open item "Onboarding process for the council").
- **Italian version** of [How members get sats](getting-sats.md).
- **Italian versions** of the checklist, the guide, the charter template and modules, and the workshop pages.
