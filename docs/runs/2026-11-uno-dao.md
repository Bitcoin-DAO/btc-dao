## Findings

1. **Council size at founding** came from the site file's placeholder seats (fixed, v0.2.1).
2. **Missing sign-up passkey** blocked the last founding step (fixed, v0.2.2).
3. **A disabled "Sign and publish"** gave no reason (fixed, v0.2.3).
4. **Gateway broadcast header** used a non-ASCII separator, shown as "Â·"; the gateway's two paths were named alike (fixed, v0.2.4).
5. **A passed rules change** did not say it waits for the council's new version (fixed, v0.2.5).
6. **Software version** on the Rules page still said 0.1.5 (fixed, v0.2.5).
7. **The trial treasury key's 12 words were never asked for.** They sit behind "Show the 12 words" on the profile; nothing at sign-up or card printing asks a council member to write them down. @CouncilC lost its phone data without them, so its treasury key is gone: the DAO runs on A and B with no spare. Wanted: council members write the words down (or, from v0.3.0, keep the key on a SeedSigner) as a required step, like printing the card.
8. **Nobody else can see that a council member's treasury key is gone.** The Council page should show it once the member says so (a lost-key flag from the member's phone, or from the replacement itself), with what it means: "2 of 3 keys left, no spare: replace C's treasury key by a council change."
9. **A lost treasury key needs a council change** (new key, new descriptor, coins moved): planned for v0.3.0 with the SeedSigner work. This run is its first real case.
10. **The profile's "type the 12 words" was a dead end when the words are lost** too (fixed, v0.2.6: it says whether the treasury still works without the key, and that a council change is the fix).
11. **"Broadcast again" is what brings a transaction to every source.** In both rounds the first broadcast was accepted but known to 2 of 3 sources; a second broadcast made it 3 of 3 and it then confirmed quickly. Proposal: the app re-sends once by itself, a minute after the broadcast, to the sources that do not know the transaction yet (related to finding 24 of the practice run).
12. **"Closed" was a small box on the home page**, and the home page still invited funding (fixed, v0.2.7: a larger notice at the top of every page; no funding card).
13. **No automated test closes a DAO.** Closing has only been tested on testnet4 (UNO practice, UNO DAO). Add it to the governance e2e.
